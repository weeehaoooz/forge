CONTENT-HUGGING VS COMPACT VS LARGE ENTERPRISE FORMS

Content-Hugging (size: 'auto')

Perfect when modal content is dynamic. Wraps tightly around children and animates naturally as items are added or removed.

Standard Form (size: 'md' ~560px)

Standard width for user profiles, setting edits, and standard single-column forms with clear validation.

Large Form (size: 'lg' / 'xl' ~768-1024px)

Multi-column grid inputs for complex infrastructure provisioning, nested grids, and resource management.

Full 100vw x 100vh modal layout for comprehensive wizards, data editors, and immersive workflows.
