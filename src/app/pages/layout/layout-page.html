How It Works

openRightPanel()

Dynamically injects any standalone component into the layout's right panel. Pass component inputs as the second argument.

mode: 'overlay'

Panel floats over the main content. Optional blurBackdrop applies a frosted glass effect.

mode: 'inline'

Panel pushes and shrinks the main content area to share horizontal space.

toggleMainContentExpanded()

Expands or resets the container padding for a full-bleed content experience.
